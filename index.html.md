# CHØs3N

Ten tracks, thirty-two minutes. An artist finding out what he sounds like in public.

Late light on sun-warmed brick, a navy cap tipped low over a grin he can't hold back. The frame is loose and unforced, and that is the point: JMØNEY isn't performing certainty. He is moving through it.

CHØs3N runs ten tracks and thirty-two minutes of hip-hop that won't settle into one lane. Released September 13, 2026 through Abstract Music, it is the sound of an artist early in his run, testing ideas out loud.

The title carries the argument. Swap the O for a Ø and the E for a 3, and "chosen" stops being a claim handed down and becomes something he wrote himself. Out now on all platforms, *CHØs3N* is the answer to a question nobody asked out loud.

## The sound

CHØs3N sits in hip-hop and rap, leaning alternative and experimental. Nothing here is built to match a template. JMØNEY treats the album as a working room, trying shapes, switching approaches, and keeping what feels like his own.

That search is audible in the project's design as much as its genre. Track titles are bent into new spellings, and six artists step in across the run. It reads as a deliberate refusal to sound like anyone else's first draft.

## The story

The album follows an artist who has only just put his feet on the music scene. There is no polished legend to protect, only a sound still being worked out and an urge to grow past it.

That is where the experimentation comes from. Rather than hide the process, CHØs3N keeps it in the frame. It is a record about becoming, made while the becoming is still happening.
